# Shadow Poster

Shadow Poster is a private tool. It publishes posts that its owner wrote to the owner's own LinkedIn profile. It has no other users.

# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

## LinkedIn API

Shadow Poster uses the LinkedIn API through the products "Share on LinkedIn" and "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect". LinkedIn's handling of your data is covered by the [LinkedIn Privacy Policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## What the app accesses

- **Share on LinkedIn** (`w_member_social`): to publish posts, and comments on those posts, to the owner's own profile.
- **Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect** (`openid`, `profile`): to read the owner's member ID and name, so each post goes to the right profile.

The app does not read the owner's feed, connections, messages or email address.

## How data is stored

- The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It stops working after 60 days and the owner signs in again.
- The text and schedule of the posts are stored in a local database on the same computer.
- No LinkedIn data is sent to any server other than LinkedIn's.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for ads.
- The app collects no analytics and uses no cookies.

## How to remove access

You can remove the app's access at any time in LinkedIn under Settings & Privacy, Data privacy, Permitted services. Deleting the stored token on the computer also removes it.
